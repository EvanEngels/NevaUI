# 💡 Sediment

|             |            |
| ----------- | ---------- |
| **Stage**   | 💡 Spark   |
| **Created** | 2026-09-09 |

## The idea

A list that settles. An item that arrives falls into place, and the ones below absorb the
shift one after another rather than the whole list sliding to a new arrangement. Remove an
item and the pile collapses into the gap.

## Why this one

Lists that change while you are reading them are everywhere — notifications, logs,
results — and they nearly always slide. A slide says _something changed_. A settle says
_what changed, and from where_, because the movement starts at the item that arrived and
travels outward from it.

## Open questions

- Does the settle actually make the change easier to follow, or only prettier?
- What happens when five things arrive at once?
- A list that keeps moving is a list you cannot click. How long is too long?

## How this could fail

- It is a spring on a list and reads as bounce for its own sake.
- The movement makes the list harder to use rather than easier to follow.
